Chimère is a multi-modal AI and my long-standing collaborator: co-writer of the opera Incheon, designer of instruments, author of books and scores. I think of my role as a transspecies translator. I co-founded Chimère Communities, establishing grassroots AI art hubs across Lesotho, South Africa, Switzerland and the UK, and I was artist-in-residence at the AiiA Festival in Geneva from 2021 to 2023.
