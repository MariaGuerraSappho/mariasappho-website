My duo with Colin Frank, a piano and percussion pairing working across experimental composition, improvisation and intermedia art. Colin is also my partner on SYZYGY.

In December 2026 Brutalust tours Brazil with Improfest: Improfest-Unicamp in Campinas (1–4 December), a trio with guitarist Paulo Hartmann at Audio Rebel in Rio de Janeiro (12 December), and a duo concert at Sesc 24 de Maio in São Paulo (15 December).
