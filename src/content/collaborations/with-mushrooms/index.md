Mushrooms are core collaborators. Their communicative networks exhibit spiking patterns similar to language, and they offer lessons in collective flourishing and alternative social practice. Working with electrodermal needles in fungal stalks, I build instruments that give them sonic agency in a space, a practice I call transspecies listening.
