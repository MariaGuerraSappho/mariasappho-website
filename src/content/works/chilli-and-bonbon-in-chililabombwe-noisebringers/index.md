The Noisebringers and Colin Frank play the fifth piece of Brice Catherin’s Zombie in Zambia collection, commissioned by Art Zoyd Studios, for any instruments and live electronics controlled by the players’ sound. Single takes, no editing, recorded in Huddersfield on 23 July 2022: percussion, cello, recorders, piano, harp, voice, pipe organ, objects.
