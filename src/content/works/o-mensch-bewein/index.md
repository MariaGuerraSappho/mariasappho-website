By Mariabrice Sapphocatherin, for organist and assistant. Based on the melody used in Bach’s BWV 622, played in five minutes across as many octaves as your hands allow, while the assistant works with the organ’s blower. Its full title translates as O man, bewail thy artificial consumption so great.
