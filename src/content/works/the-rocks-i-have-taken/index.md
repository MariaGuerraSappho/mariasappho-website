Rock carvings, collected land, Venus. The series has a companion album, a visual album of stories, sounds and videos of rocks. Each rock is paired with a story about a land that has felt human invasion, from Brooklyn to Dachau to Tasmania, through exchanged histories, conquered lands and lost languages. With Alessia Anastassopulos, Ronan Whittern, Dejana Sekulic, Brice Catherin, Katie Oswell, Henry McPherson and Colin Frank.
