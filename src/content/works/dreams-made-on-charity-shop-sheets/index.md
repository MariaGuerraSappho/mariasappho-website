A crocheted tube about 30 metres long, made from bed sheets bought in charity shops, with a piezoelectric cable running through its whole length. When the audience picks it up and shakes it together, their movement becomes sound.

Premiered at the Glasgow Improvisers Orchestra Festival at CCA Glasgow, with the Naga Mas Gamelan ensemble and the Perempuan Komponis collective.
