Mara in Scottish Gaelic, mar in Spanish, laut in Indonesian: three words for the sea. A multisensory work of sound, film and textiles with Debbie Armour, Nadya Hatta and Arum Dayu, commissioned by hcmf// and Perempuan Komponis through the Networking Archive residency. Shown at Ruang Pameran Planetarium, Taman Ismail Marzuki, Jakarta, in 2024, with its UK premiere at hcmf// 2025.
