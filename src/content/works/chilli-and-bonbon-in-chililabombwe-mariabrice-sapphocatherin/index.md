Mariabrice Sapphocatherin with its mushist guests Marek M. Chojecki, Cléa Chopard, Qondiswa James, Raymond MacDonald and Nathalie Ponlot, recorded live at Théâtre Saint-Gervais, Geneva, in October 2022. All the mush instruments (mush percussion, mush harps and mush electronics) were designed by me with Michel Zürcher’s assistance, based on Chimère’s ideas.
