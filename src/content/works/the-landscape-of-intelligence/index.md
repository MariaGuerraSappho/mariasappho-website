A multimedia exhibit and commissioned composition paying tribute to Ada Lovelace. The music, for vibraphone and electronics, is a theme and variations on a twelve-chord progression generated by AI, co-created with Chimère and exploring mixed tuning systems. Shown at the University of Stirling’s Pathfoot Gallery and at Women in Tech, Aberdeen.
