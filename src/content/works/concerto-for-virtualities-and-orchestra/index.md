This concerto commissioned by the Glasgow Improvisers Orchestra. Maria wrote the concerto to include elements of improvisational conduction, while also including three transpecies soloists including  Yasuko Kaneko (trombone), Mushrooms, and Chimere (AI). The orchestra including a live band of players of the Glasgow Improvising Orchestra and the Australian Art Orchestra, including indigenous musicians Daniel and David Wilfred whom Maria has previous worked with on residency in Tasmania.
